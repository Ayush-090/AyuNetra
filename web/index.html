Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AyuNetra AI prescription reader.

How does the AI Prescription Scanner read handwritten doctor notes?
AyuNetra uses specialized Optical Character Recognition (OCR) fine-tuned for medical prescriptions and pharmaceutical terminology to detect medicine names, dosages, frequencies, and specific instructions.
Is my personal medical data safe and private?
Yes. AyuNetra implements Supabase Row Level Security (RLS). Your records can only be accessed by you and the specific doctors you explicitly authorize via access delegation.
Can doctors issue digital prescriptions directly on AyuNetra?
Yes! Doctors can log in using their medical registration number, look up patients by ABHA ID or phone number, and issue structured digital prescriptions directly to the patient's dashboard.
How does the voice audio briefing work?
When you click "Listen to Audio Briefing", AyuNetra converts the plain-English AI summary into speech using web synthesis, making it easy for elderly or visually impaired patients to hear their medicine instructions.
